## The brief

TYPE01 is a magazine for modern creatives, and in March it hosts its very first conference: two days in London about typography in print, motion design and branding. Friday is for workshops, Saturday for talks by seven speakers with Tobias Frere-Jones as keynote, followed by a conference dinner. The Saturday talks can also be followed through a free livestream.

The task was to design the conference website from the content the client provided, for desktop and phone, and to decide myself how to organise it.

## What I did

I split the content into a homepage and pages for the speakers (with a detail page per speaker), the Friday and Saturday schedule and practical info. The homepage reads like a programme: the event, top speakers, tickets, the venue, accommodation, the livestream and the partners. Buying a ticket or signing up for the stream is never more than one tap away.

The style is louder than the magazine, but still TYPE01: black, red and grey, a pixel typeface, and tape with speaker names and topics running across the hero. The tickets look like real tickets, with a barcode and a punched edge.
