## The brief

*A world of flavours in every box.* Miles & Meals is a subscription box that brings iconic street food from one city at a time to your kitchen. I designed the visual identity, presented in a brand board, and the companion app where subscribers manage their box and browse recipes.

## What I did

Competitors like HelloFresh and Gousto sell convenience. Miles & Meals sells travel, culture and local stories, so I built the brand on authenticity, connection and discovery.

The result is a bold yellow wordmark on warm red, with a pattern of loose strokes that reads like an abstract city map. Each box type (Local Legends, Street Made, Neighbourhood Bakes) gets its own colour, so every edition feels different but still belongs to one brand. The app carries the same shapes and colours into every screen, from city guides to recipes.
